Quick glossary

Projected actual return
Coinbase test × 0.55 (matching live strategy fee check 282%→158%). Robinhood × 0.90. Coinbase higher-fee line × 0.24.
Test return
Yearly growth Jan 2023 – Mar 2025, held-back data.
Goal chance
Share of simulated replays that reached the account's growth goal (Coinbase: $569 → $1M by Oct 2027; Robinhood: about +1% a week).
Consistency bar
Made money in both halves of the older data (2016–2019 and 2020–2022) and does not use 3x funds.
Worst drop
Biggest peak-to-trough fall in the test.
Risk-adjusted score
Return per unit of bumpiness; above 1 good, above 2 strong.
Watch list
Passed the lab's tests and is being tracked, but no money (paper or real) is on it yet.

How to read this board

Goal chance
How often the strategy reached its account's growth goal across many simulated replays of its test results.
Test return
Yearly growth in the test period.
Projected actual return
Test return cut down for real fees/slippage.
Worst drop
Biggest fall from a high during the test.
Vs holding
Buy-and-hold of the same coin/fund.
