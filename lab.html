Quick glossary

Projected actual return
Coinbase test × 0.55 (matching live strategy fee check 282%→158%). Robinhood × 0.90. Higher-fee line × 0.24.
Test return
Yearly growth Jan 2023 – Mar 2025, held-back data.
Goal chance
Share of simulated replays that reached the desk's growth goal.
Worst drop
Biggest peak-to-trough fall in the test.
Risk-adjusted score
Return per unit of bumpiness; above 1 good, above 2 strong.
